Give visitors a sensible ballpark and send your office a better-qualified estimate request.

Many customers won't call until they have a rough idea of cost. A calculator built from your own pricing rules gives them a clearly labeled preliminary range, explains what isn't included, and sends you their details and project specifics before you pick up the phone.

How it works

Your prices, your rules, your final say.

Built from your pricing rules

We sit down with you and write out how you actually price a typical job: base rates, what makes it cost more, and your minimums. You approve every number. The tool never invents prices.

Clearly preliminary

Customers see a range, not a quote, labeled as preliminary, with a short list of what isn't included and what could change the price.

A person gives the final quote

The calculator ends with a request for an on-site or phone estimate. Your office gets the customer's details and answers by email or in your CRM.

What the calculator won't do

  • Make up prices or use "industry averages" you haven't approved
  • Present a range as a firm quote
  • Hide exclusions or conditions that change the price
  • Replace your site visit or your judgment
  • Try to cover every service at once. We start with one and expand if it works for you
Process

From your price sheet to a working tool.

  1. Pick one service

    Choose the job customers most often ask "how much?" about, such as roof replacement, water heater install or furnace replacement.

  2. Write down your rules

    We work out your pricing rules, ranges and exclusions with you. You approve every number and every word.

  3. Build, test, launch

    We build it, you test it with real past jobs to make sure the ranges look right, then it goes live. Requests go to your email or CRM.
