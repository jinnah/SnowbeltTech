What local SEO means here

Useful pages and accurate information, not tricks.

For a contractor, local SEO mostly comes down to three things: does your site clearly explain each service you offer, does it make clear where you work, and does the information match everywhere it appears online. That's the work.

Service pages

A clear page for each main service. It explains what you do, who it's for, what affects the price, and how to request a quote.

Location information

Accurate details about where you work, consistent with your Google Business Profile, plus location pages only where you have something specific to say.

Structure & links

Page titles, headings and internal links that help both customers and Google understand how your services fit together.

How we measure

Visits and inquiries, not vanity rankings.

Ranking #1 for a phrase nobody searches doesn't pay the bills. We track what does: how many people found your site through search, and how many of them called or asked for a quote.

We track

  • Visits from Google search and Maps
  • Calls and quote requests from those visits
  • Google Business Profile calls and clicks

We don't sell

  • Guaranteed rankings
  • Keyword-position reports as "results"
  • Monthly link packages

What we won't do

  • Mass-produce near-identical pages that swap in town names
  • Stuff keywords into pages until they read badly
  • Buy links or join link schemes
  • Promise a #1 ranking or a guaranteed position
  • Add fake locations, reviews or credentials

These tactics can get a site or listing penalized, and they rarely bring in the customers you want.

Questions

Local SEO FAQ

Your Google listing matters as much as your site. See Google Business Profile management.

Can you work on a site you didn't build?

Usually. If your platform lets us edit pages (WordPress, Wix, Squarespace and most custom sites do), we can carry out the implementation package. If it doesn't, the analysis package still gives you a clear plan.

How is this different from the monthly SEO report?

The monthly report tells you what we found. The analysis package is a deeper, one-time review with a full plan. The implementation package is us doing the work.

Should I have a page for every town I serve?

Only where you have something genuinely different to say, like projects you've done there or details specific to that area. Dozens of copy-paste town pages tend to hurt more than they help.

How long until I see results?

It varies with your market and starting point. Fixes to pages and local information usually show up over weeks to a few months. We'll show you what changed and track visits and inquiries along the way.
