    Common questions

    Do you need to have built my website to monitor it?

    No. Monitoring and reports work on almost any public website, including WordPress, Wix, Squarespace, Shopify and custom builds. For uptime, speed, SSL and link checks we only need your web address.

    What do the AI agents actually do?

    They run scheduled checks on your site, re-test anything that fails to rule out false alarms, and write a first draft of your monthly report. A person reviews every alert and report. The agents never change your website.

    Will you fix the problems you find?

    If you want us to. Fixes aren't included in monitoring, so you get a clear quote first. You're also welcome to send the report to your current web person.

    Do I need to give you my passwords?

    Not for monitoring. Testing your contact form needs nothing beyond the form itself. Some SEO checks go deeper if you choose to give us read-only access to Google Search Console or your Business Profile.

    What's in the free health check?

    A one-time look at your site's speed, SSL and security basics, mobile experience, contact form and top SEO issues. We send it to you in 1–2 business days. You're under no obligation.

    Is there a contract?

    Monthly plans are month to month. Website builds are a one-time project with a written scope and price before we start.
