    Common questions

    Is the demo site really free?

    Yes. We design a working demo of your new website at no cost and send you a private link. If you don't love it, you don't owe us anything.

    What does "unlimited revisions" mean?

    Before launch, you can ask for as many changes to your design as you like: colors, wording, photos, layout, pages. We keep refining until you're happy with it. Brand-new features beyond the agreed scope get quoted separately, and we'll always tell you upfront.

    Do I have to sign up for monitoring?

    No. Monitoring, reports and care plans are optional add-ons. Your website is yours either way.

    Can you guarantee I'll rank #1 on Google?

    No, and be careful with anyone who does. We make your Google listing and website accurate, complete and useful, then measure the calls and inquiries they bring in.

    Will an estimate calculator give my customers exact prices?

    No. It shows a clearly labeled preliminary range based on pricing rules you approve, lists what's not included, and sends the request to your office. A person on your team always gives the final quote.

    Do you need to have built my website to help?

    No. Monitoring, reports, Google Business Profile work and local SEO all work on most existing sites, including WordPress, Wix, Squarespace and custom builds.

    What's in the free health check?

    A one-time look at your site's speed, SSL and security basics, mobile experience, contact form and top SEO issues. We send it to you in 1–2 business days. You're under no obligation.
