Who it's for

Contractors and local service businesses.

Roofers, plumbers, HVAC and electrical contractors, landscapers, cleaners, remodelers, and anyone who serves customers in Buffalo and Western New York, with or without a storefront. If you work out of a truck and serve customers at their homes, Google treats you as a service-area business, and we set your listing up that way.

Your listing is wrong or incomplete

Old hours, a missing service, the wrong category, a phone number that goes nowhere. Small errors quietly cost you calls.

You're not sure who controls it

A former employee or an old agency set it up and nobody has the login. We help you get ownership back through Google's own process.

You don't have time to keep it fresh

New photos, project updates and review replies take time you'd rather spend on the job. We handle it, with your approval.

What we do

Real implementation, not just a list of suggestions.

Our monthly SEO report already looks at your profile. This service is different: we make the changes.

Ownership & eligibility review

We confirm who owns the listing, check that it meets Google's guidelines, and look for duplicates or old listings that confuse customers.

Categories, services, hours & service area

We correct your primary and secondary categories, list the services you actually offer, set accurate hours and holiday hours, and define the area you really serve.

Real photos & factual updates

We help you add genuine project photos and short, factual updates, such as a finished job, a seasonal service, or a new offering. Never stock photos passed off as your work.

Review responses you approve

We draft polite, specific replies to new reviews, good and bad. You approve them before anything is posted under your name.

Listing corrections

Fixing a wrong address or phone number, merging or removing duplicates, and other appropriate corrections, through Google's standard processes.

Basic reporting

A short monthly summary from Google's own profile data: calls, website clicks and direction requests, plus what we changed and why.

How we work

Honest listings last. Shortcuts get suspended.

Google regularly suspends listings that break its rules, and a suspended listing can take weeks to come back. We only do things we'd be comfortable explaining to Google.

We can't promise a particular ranking. No one honestly can. What we can promise is a listing that's accurate, complete and kept up to date, with reporting on the calls and clicks it actually brings in.

What we won't do

  • Promise a #1 spot or "guaranteed" rankings
  • Create fake locations, virtual offices or PO-box addresses
  • Stuff keywords or city names into your business name
  • Buy, write or swap fake reviews
  • Offer discounts in exchange for reviews, or filter out unhappy customers
  • Post anything under your name without your OK

Questions

Google Business Profile FAQ

Want this paired with your website? See local SEO, or get a free demo site.

Do I have to give you my Google password?

No. You add us as a manager on your profile, and you stay the owner. You can remove our access at any time.

I don't have a storefront. Can I still have a listing?

Yes. Most contractors are "service-area businesses". We set the listing up to show the area you serve and hide your home address, which is what Google asks for.

My listing is suspended or I can't get into it. Can you help?

Usually. We'll work through Google's reinstatement or ownership request process with you. Google makes the final decision, so we can't guarantee the outcome or timing.

Will this get me more reviews?

We can help you set up an easy, honest way to ask happy customers for a review, such as a short link to text after a job. We don't buy reviews, incentivize them, or screen out unhappy customers.

How will I know it's working?

Each month you'll see the calls, website clicks and direction requests your profile brought in, from Google's own data, alongside what we changed.
